The brief
Hardware + API + Grok
Pick a piece of hardware, an API, connect it to Grok (the API, Grok Voice, or the bot framework), and by Thursday afternoon show something that didn't work when you started.
You can use
Grok API
Grok 4.7 for chat, reasoning and code, with function calling and structured outputs.(opens in a new tab)
Search and code tools
Web search, X search, code execution and search over your own documents, built into the API.(opens in a new tab)
Grok Voice
Real-time speech-to-speech agents, text to speech and speech to text.(opens in a new tab)
Grok Imagine
Generate and edit images and videos from text.(opens in a new tab)
Grok Build
A coding agent for your terminal, also usable in scripts and from editors.(opens in a new tab)
Grok Bot
AI teammates with their own computer that work across your apps.(opens in a new tab)
Cursor
A coding agent for the desktop and the terminal, with parallel cloud agents and Grok 4.7 among its models.(opens in a new tab)

Teams
Three ways to take part
1Register a team
Sign in, create your team (name and a short description) and you immediately get its secret code. It stays visible to every member on your dashboard. Share it with the people you want: a team has 4 to 6 members.
2Join a team
Got a code from a teammate? Sign in, choose Join a team and enter it. If the code is wrong, no longer active, or the team is full, you get a clear message.
3I'm looking for a team
On your own? Mark yourself as looking for a team and add how to reach you. Teams with room can see your name, profile and note, and send you their code.
The rules
01Sign in with your Luma email
Use the same email you used to register on Luma. Only guests the organisers have approved on Luma can sign in (not waitlist or pending); we send a one-time code to that address.
02Register a team, join one, or look for one
Register a team and get its secret code, join a team by entering the code a teammate shares with you, or register as looking for a team so teams with room can find you. Registering a team gives priority for participation confirmation.
03Teams of 4 to 6
A team needs at least 4 people to count as formed. At 6 members it closes automatically and the code stops working; if someone leaves, it opens again.
04One team at a time, and you can leave
You can be in one team only. You can leave whenever you want before the lock. If the person who created the team leaves, the earliest-joined member takes over; a team with nobody left is removed.
05Teams lock on 2 November 2026 at 23:59 (Rome time)
This is also the end of registration. After that moment nobody can register, join or leave a team. Sort out your team before then.
06Guaranteed seats for teams of 4+
When teams lock, every member of a team with 4 or more people has a guaranteed seat. Everyone else (smaller teams, solo, available) gets the remaining seats, up to 100 in total, first come, first served by Luma registration time.
