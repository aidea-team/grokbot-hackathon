## Il brief (testo originale)

> Some of you were at the Café Cursor evenings in Milano. This one is a bit different.
> ctrlalt.museum is a retro computing museum in Pavia run by comPVter, a nonprofit I've been involved with for years. They have a robotic dog, a driving simulator, a flight simulator, and a few drones, and they're letting us hack on them for two days, actual access, not a demo behind a rope.
> The brief: pick a piece of hardware, an API, connect it to Grok (the API, Grok Voice, or the bot framework), and by Thursday afternoon show us something that didn't work when you started.
